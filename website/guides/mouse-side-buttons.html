How to use your mouse's side buttons as shortcuts on Windows

Updated 5 November 2026 · Windows 10 and 11

Most mice have two buttons on the side that you've probably never pressed. In File Explorer and your browser, Back goes back a folder or a page and Forward goes forward again. You can make them do much more.

How to do it

  1. Get OrbitDrop, free on the Microsoft Store (version 0.7 or later).
  2. Open OrbitDrop's Settings (its icon is next to the clock) › Shortcuts and click Keyboard shortcut.
  3. Press your side button. Done.
  4. Now select a photo, a PDF or a video and press the button: the wheel opens right there. Click PNG, WebP, PDF or whatever you need.

Your browser won't go back too

The press belongs to OrbitDrop, so the page doesn't also go back. The other side button keeps working as usual.

No side buttons?

Ctrl or Alt with the middle button (the scroll wheel) works too, and so do the extra keys F13 to F24 on their own.

More for one button

In the Microsoft Store version, a button can also open your Dock of favourite apps, your Scenes or a Media wheel for your music.

Questions

Do I need my mouse's own software?

No. It works with any mouse that has side buttons, without the maker's app.

Can I undo it?

Yes: Settings › Shortcuts, click the shortcut and press another key, or Reset.
