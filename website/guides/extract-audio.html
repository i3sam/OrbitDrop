How to get the MP3 out of a video on Windows

Updated 5 November 2026 · Windows 10 and 11

Want just the music, a voice memo or a lecture's audio from a video? One move turns it into an MP3.

How to do it

  1. Get OrbitDrop, free on the Microsoft Store.
  2. Start dragging the file in File Explorer and, while you're still holding it, tap Shift. A wheel opens under your pointer. Let go on MP3.
  3. The MP3 is saved next to the video. Nothing is uploaded: OrbitDrop works on your PC, even offline.

Converting audio files

Drag an audio file and the wheel offers MP3, M4A, WAV, WMA and FLAC. Handy for a WAV that's too big, or an M4A an old player won't read.

Lots of videos

Select them all and do the same: each one gets its own MP3.

Questions

Is the quality good?

Yes, the sound is saved as a high-quality MP3.

Can I do this with a YouTube video?

Only with a video file that's on your PC and that you have the right to use.
