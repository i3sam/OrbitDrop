How to merge PDF files on Windows for free, offline

Updated 5 November 2026 · Windows 10 and 11

Windows can't merge PDFs on its own, and uploading contracts or bank statements to a random website isn't great. You can do it on your PC instead.

How to do it

  1. Get OrbitDrop, free on the Microsoft Store.
  2. Select the PDFs in File Explorer and start dragging them.
  3. Tap Shift twice for Tools and let go on Merge. One combined PDF appears in the same folder, in the order you picked the files.

Split, compress or rotate

The same Tools wheel splits a PDF into pages, compresses it for email and rotates it. Drag one PDF, tap Shift twice and choose.

Photos or scans into one PDF

Select several images (JPG, PNG, HEIC…) and turn them into a single PDF: handy for documents you photographed with your phone.

Why not a website?

Nothing is uploaded: OrbitDrop works on your PC, even offline. That matters for IDs, contracts and anything private.

Questions

Is there a limit on files or pages?

No sign-up, no page limits and no watermark.

Do I need Adobe Acrobat?

No. OrbitDrop merges, splits, compresses and rotates PDFs by itself.
