How to get hot corners on Windows 11

Updated 11 November 2026 · Windows 10 and 11

Coming from a Mac, you might miss hot corners: push the pointer into a corner and something happens. Windows 11 doesn't have them built in, but you can get close.

How to do it

  1. Get OrbitDrop from the Microsoft Store.
  2. Open Settings › Screen corners and choose what each corner does with files: OneDrive, a USB stick, a folder, Print or Share.
  3. Drag a file towards a corner. It lights up and says where the file goes. Let go.

What Windows has

The far right end of the taskbar shows the desktop when you click it (Settings › Personalization › Taskbar › Taskbar behaviors). Win + Tab shows all your windows. That's as close as Windows gets on its own.

A corner that opens an app

With OrbitDrop's corner app, move the pointer into the bottom right corner, just above the clock, and click the Open Notepad button that appears. Pick any app or folder instead in Settings › Screen corners. More about Screen corners.

Mac-style hot corners

If you want the pointer alone to trigger things (show the desktop, Task View, the screen saver), the free, open-source WinXCorners does that, and it works alongside OrbitDrop.

Questions

Will corners get in the way of my windows' buttons?

No. OrbitDrop's corners only appear while you drag a file, and the corner app sits above the clock.

Do they work in games?

They never show over a game, a full-screen video or the lock screen.
