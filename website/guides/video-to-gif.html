How to turn a video into a GIF on Windows

Updated 5 November 2026 · Windows 10 and 11

A short clip as a GIF plays everywhere: chats, slides, documentation. No editor, no website, no watermark.

How to do it

  1. Get OrbitDrop, free on the Microsoft Store.
  2. Start dragging the file in File Explorer and, while you're still holding it, tap Shift. A wheel opens under your pointer. Let go on GIF.
  3. OrbitDrop makes an animated GIF of the first 10 seconds, next to your video.

Which videos work?

MP4, MOV, WMV and the other videos Windows can play. If a video uses a format Windows can't read (like ProRes), OrbitDrop tells you which one.

Other things you can do with a video

The same wheel converts it to MP4 or WMV, takes the sound out as MP3, and (with Shift tapped twice) compresses it to 1080p, 720p or 480p.

Questions

Why only 10 seconds?

GIFs get very big very fast. Ten seconds keeps them small enough to send. Trim your clip first if you want a different part.

Is there a watermark?

No.
