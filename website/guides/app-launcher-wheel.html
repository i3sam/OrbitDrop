Open your favourite apps from one key on Windows

Updated 5 November 2026 · Windows 10 and 11

The taskbar fills up, the Start menu is a search away. OrbitDrop's Dock puts the things you open every day in a circle right where your pointer is: press a key, point, click.

How to do it

  1. Get OrbitDrop from the Microsoft Store.
  2. Open Settings › Dock. A short step-by-step setup helps you add apps, websites and folders, and pick your own key.
  3. Press Ctrl + Alt + D anywhere. Point at what you want and click: it opens.

Apps, websites and folders

Add any installed app, any website (it shows the site's own icon and opens in your browser) and any folder. Drag them around the big wheel in Settings to arrange them.

Groups

Need more? Make a group: one slot that opens a wheel of its own, with up to 8 more apps, like all your games or all your work tools.

A Media wheel for your music

Press Ctrl + Alt + M for play, pause, skip and volume, with the song that's playing in the middle. It works with Spotify, YouTube in your browser and other players.

One key for everything

Turn on One key for everything: OrbitDrop's wheel key opens your Dock when no file is selected. Press it again for Scenes, then Media. It can even be a side button on your mouse.

Free to try

The Dock is part of the OrbitDrop upgrade in the Microsoft Store version. You can try it free for 3 days first. Everything else in OrbitDrop stays free.

Questions

Does it slow my PC down?

No. OrbitDrop waits in the notification area and only wakes up when you press its keys.

Can I change the key?

Yes, to any combination you like, or a side button on your mouse.
