Set up a gaming mode on Windows in one key

Updated 5 November 2026 · Windows 10 and 11

Before every game: close the browser and the chat apps, switch the power mode, open Game Bar, start the launcher. OrbitDrop's Scenes do all of it from one key, and the same for work or study.

How to do it

  1. Get OrbitDrop from the Microsoft Store.
  2. Open Settings › Scenes and follow the short setup: name a scene (Gaming, Work, Study…) and choose what it does.
  3. Press Ctrl + Alt + S, point at the scene and click.

What a scene can do

  • Close the apps you choose, like your browser, Discord or Teams.
  • Best performance: switches Windows' power mode for the most speed. It stays on until you change it.
  • Open Xbox Game Bar: pin its FPS counter once (Performance › pin) and it stays on screen in your games.
  • Open what you need: apps, websites and folders, like Steam and your Discord server, or your notes and a timer.

Work and study scenes

A Work scene can close games and open your email, calendar and project folder. A Study scene can close distractions and open your notes and course website.

Windows' own Game Mode

Windows has a Game Mode in Settings › Gaming that helps a little in the background. A scene goes further: it closes apps, changes the power mode and opens what you need, in one key.

Free to try

Scenes are part of the OrbitDrop upgrade in the Microsoft Store version, free to try for 3 days. Everything else in OrbitDrop stays free.

Questions

Will it close an app with unsaved work?

Choose which apps a scene closes. Leave out the ones you're working in.

Does Best performance stay on?

Yes, until you change Windows' power mode back in Settings › System › Power.
