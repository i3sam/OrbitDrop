How to compress a photo to under 1 MB on Windows

Updated 5 November 2026 · Windows 10 and 11

An upload form says "maximum 1 MB" and your photo is 6 MB. Here's how to get it under the limit without a website and without ruining it.

How to do it

  1. Get OrbitDrop, free on the Microsoft Store.
  2. Start dragging the photo and tap Shift twice: the wheel switches to Tools.
  3. Rest on Compress for a moment and its options open: 1 MB, 500 KB, 200 KB, or 75%, 50% or 25% of the size. Let go on the one you need.
  4. A smaller copy is saved next to the photo, named (compressed). Your original stays as it was.

Which option should I pick?

Use 1 MB, 500 KB or 200 KB when a form gives you a limit: OrbitDrop makes sure the result is under it. Use the percentages to simply make photos lighter for email or sharing.

Videos and PDFs too

Drag a video, tap Shift twice and Compress to 1080p, 720p or 480p. A PDF that's too big to email can be compressed the same way.

Questions

Will the photo look worse?

At 1 MB you usually can't tell. At 200 KB it's sized for small uploads like profile pictures and ID forms.

Can I compress many photos at once?

Yes. Select them all, start dragging and do the same: each one is compressed.
