How to convert Word to PDF (and PDF to Word) on Windows

Updated 5 November 2026 · Windows 10 and 11

You don't need to open Word, find Save as and pick PDF every time. Convert right from the folder, one file or twenty.

How to do it

  1. Get OrbitDrop, free on the Microsoft Store.
  2. Start dragging the file in File Explorer and, while you're still holding it, tap Shift. A wheel opens under your pointer. Let go on PDF.
  3. To go the other way, drag a PDF and let go on DOCX: you get a Word document you can edit.

What you need

OrbitDrop uses the Microsoft Word, Excel or PowerPoint already on your PC to make the PDF, or the free LibreOffice if you have that instead. If Word won't start (it's waiting on a sign-in or a message), OrbitDrop tries again and tells you what to do.

Excel and PowerPoint too

Spreadsheets and slides convert the same way: drag, tap Shift, let go on PDF.

The Word way

In Word: File › Save as, choose PDF under the file type. Fine for one document; slower for many.

Questions

Will the PDF look the same as my document?

Yes, because Word itself makes it: fonts, pictures and pages stay as they are.

How good is PDF to Word?

Text and layout come across well for normal documents. Scanned pages (pictures of text) need text recognition first.
