Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

OrbitDrop is an offline file utility for Windows. In short: your files never leave your PC, and OrbitDrop collects no personal data.

What OrbitDrop does with your files

  • Everything OrbitDrop does (converting, compressing, resizing, editing, reading text from images, zipping and so on) runs on your PC. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.
  • OrbitDrop only touches the files you choose with the wheel. Results are saved as new files next to the original; the original is never changed.
  • "Share" opens the Windows share window, and "Copy to" and "Move to" use folders you pick. Where your files go after that is your choice.

What OrbitDrop watches

To notice the gesture (dragging a file, then tapping Shift), OrbitDrop watches the mouse buttons and the Shift key while it runs, and listens for its keyboard shortcut, Ctrl + Alt + Space (you can turn the shortcut off in Settings → Shortcuts). It doesn't record, store or send keystrokes, mouse movements or anything you type.

When you use the shortcut, OrbitDrop asks File Explorer which files are selected, so it knows what to work on.

What OrbitDrop stores

  • Settings in %APPDATA%\OrbitDrop\settings.json: your preferences, such as theme, shortcut and wheel layout.
  • Logs in %LOCALAPPDATA%\OrbitDrop\logs: technical notes such as errors, used for troubleshooting. File names are included only if you turn that on in Settings → Advanced. Logs stay on your PC and are never sent anywhere.

Uninstalling OrbitDrop removes the app. You can delete the folders above to remove its settings and logs too.

Network access

  • Microsoft Store version: OrbitDrop makes no network requests of its own, except the one-time downloads and the picture search described below, and only when you use those tools. The Microsoft Store handles updates.
  • Version downloaded from GitHub: OrbitDrop checks GitHub for new versions (at most every few hours) and downloads updates from GitHub. That request contains only the app's name and version number. You can turn this off in Settings → About.
  • Background removal and the magic eraser (upgrade): the first time you use each, OrbitDrop downloads its model once and checks it: background removal from GitHub (about 224 MB), the magic eraser from Hugging Face (about 208 MB). Your photos are never sent anywhere: the cut-out and the erasing are done on your PC. (Upscaling comes with OrbitDrop and needs no download.)
  • Search the web for a picture (upgrade): only when you choose it, a copy of that one picture (at most 1600 pixels) is sent to the search engine you picked (Google Lens, Bing or TinEye) and its results open in your browser, under that site's own privacy policy. No other picture or information is sent.

OrbitDrop has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or telemetry, and it doesn't sell or share any data. This website doesn't use cookies or analytics either.

The OrbitDrop upgrade and tips

OrbitDrop is free. The optional upgrade (extra tools, one payment) and tips are only offered in the Microsoft Store version, and are bought through the Microsoft Store: Microsoft handles the payment, and OrbitDrop only asks the Store whether the upgrade is owned. OrbitDrop never sees your payment details, name or email address.

The feedback form on this website

If you use the help & feedback page to get help, report a bug or request a feature, what you type there (and your email address, only if you choose to give it) is sent as a private email to the developer, through the email service Resend. It isn't stored on this website, isn't published, and is only used to read your feedback and, if you asked for one, to reply. Your IP address is used for a few minutes to stop the form being flooded, and isn't stored. Please don't put passwords or other sensitive information in the form. To have an email you sent deleted, send another note through the form saying so.

The waitlist on the setup page

If you join the list on the setup page, your email address is stored with the email service Resend so the developer can send you new parts of the series and news about OrbitDrop's new features. It's used for nothing else, never sold or shared, and every email has a way to leave the list; you can also ask to be removed through the feedback page. The ticks you set on that page stay in your own browser and are never sent anywhere.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The feedback form only receives what someone chooses to type into it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
